PART 1 Alexa: Super Admin Objective 1

1.1 Log in and dashboard

  1. Open the login page and sign in as alexamae.villalon.cics@ust.edu.ph.
  2. Show the Super Admin dashboard: totals by role, assessment activity chart, recent logs.
Alexa: I'm logged in as the Super Admin. BarBuddy uses role-based access: each user only sees the portal for their role. This dashboard shows platform statistics such as the number of reviewees and administrators, assessment activity, and the latest system logs.

1.2 Promote Eis to Review Administrator

  1. Go to User Control. Point out the loading skeleton, the KPI tiles, search and role filter.
  2. Find Eis's account (currently a Reviewee) and click Edit (pencil).
  3. Change the role to Review Administrator (Lawyer), click Save Changes, then confirm Yes, save changes in the confirmation dialog.
Alexa: Every new account registers as a reviewee by default. As Super Admin, I can assign the Review Administrator role. Here I'm promoting Eis's account. Notice that every important change asks for confirmation first.

1.3 Invite, bulk invite, report and archive

  1. Click Invite User: show the form (name, email, role). Optionally send one to a team email; it arrives with a Register Now link.
  2. Click Bulk Invite (CSV) and then Download template to show the CSV format. Close the dialog.
  3. Click Download Users Report: a CSV with totals per role and every user's role, status and package.
  4. Click Archive on a test account to show the confirmation, then click Cancel.
Alexa: I can invite users one at a time or in bulk from a CSV file. Invited users receive an email that links them to the registration page with their role already assigned. I can also download a users report, and archive or delete accounts. Super Admin accounts are protected.

1.4 Announcement, settings and logs

  1. Announcements: create one (e.g. "Welcome to BarBuddy review") for Reviewees and Publish it. Yko will see it in notifications.
  2. Settings, Assessment Settings: show the daily, practice and Mock Bar settings. Under Mock Bar Unlock Rule, set the streak to 1 day for the demo and save. Mention the default is Level 4 or a 30-day streak.
  3. System Logs: show the log of everything just done (role change, invite, announcement), with search and filters.
Alexa: Administrators can post announcements, configure the number of questions and time limits, and set the rule that unlocks the Mock Bar Examination. Every action in the system is recorded in the system logs for auditing. I'll now pass it to Eis, who is now a Review Administrator.

PART 2 Eis: Review Administrator (Content) Objectives 1 and 2

2.1 Log in and dashboard

  1. Log in with Eis's account. It now opens the Review Administrator dashboard because of Alexa's promotion.
  2. Show the dashboard summary: question bank per subject, syllabus documents, verification queue.
Eis: Because Alexa assigned me the Review Administrator role, my account now opens the Review Administrator portal. From here we manage the content that BarBuddy uses to assess reviewees.

2.2 Question bank

  1. Go to Questionnaire and click Upload CSV. Choose the prepared CSV and show the review step, which flags duplicates and invalid rows.
  2. Confirm the import (Yes, import).
  3. Open a subject's question bank: search, paginate, expand a question to show its lawyer-approved model answer.
  4. Add a question manually (subject, question, model answer), confirm, then show Archive and Restore.
Eis: The question bank holds Bar questions with their lawyer-approved model answers, more than one thousand three hundred so far. We can import questions from a CSV file; BarBuddy checks for duplicates and missing fields before importing. These model answers are what the AI uses to evaluate the reviewees' essays.

2.3 Syllabus

  1. Go to Curriculum and click upload. Enter a title, choose the syllabus PDF, confirm.
  2. Show View (opens the PDF), Edit (rename the title), and Archive / Restore with confirmations.
Eis: The Bar syllabus is uploaded as one complete PDF. Review Administrators can upload, rename, replace, archive or delete it, and every reviewee can read and download it. Now Yko will show the reviewee side.

PART 3 Yko: Reviewee Objectives 1, 2, 3 and 4

3.1 Registration and login

  1. Open the landing page: features, and pricing for Packages 1 to 4 (7, 14, 45 and 65 days).
  2. Click Get Started. Fill in the registration form and show the validations (password rules, contact number).
  3. Click Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to open them, then tick the agreement and Create Account.
  4. Open the inbox: the branded BarBuddy confirmation email. Confirm, then log in.
Yko: This is the reviewee side. New users register with their email, which is verified through a confirmation email. New accounts are reviewees by default.

3.2 Choose a package and receive the daily questions

  1. After logging in, BarBuddy asks the reviewee to choose a review package. Pick one (e.g. Package 4, 7 days) and confirm.
  2. Open the inbox: the "BarBuddy Review Partner" daily questions email with Start Answering.
Yko: Online payment isn't enabled yet, so reviewees choose a package directly. The package decides which Bar subjects I review. Every day at 7 AM, BarBuddy releases three new questions from my subjects and emails them to me. Questions are never repeated. For this demo, the first set is sent right away.

3.3 Answering

  1. Click Start Answering. Show the reading period, the countdown timer, and the one-page layout (question, then answer box).
  2. Write a good answer for one question (ALAC: Answer, Legal Basis, Application, Conclusion) and a short answer for another. Show auto-save and the flag button.
  3. Click Submit and confirm in the dialog.
Yko: The exam simulates the Bar: there's a reading period, a timer, and my answers are saved automatically, even if my connection drops. When time runs out, the answers are submitted automatically.

3.4 Results and AI comparison

  1. On the results page, wait a few seconds for the AI Comparison to appear.
  2. Show for each essay: the suggested (model) answer, the AI verdict (Matches / Partially Matches / Does Not Match), the five-point score, the ALAC breakdown, and the reminder about not relying entirely on AI.
Yko: After submitting, BarBuddy's AI, powered by Google Gemini, compares my answer only against the lawyer-approved model answer. It gives a score based on the Supreme Court's five-point grading standard and rates each ALAC component. A Review Administrator then checks every AI result.

3.5 Dashboard, subjects, syllabus and engagement

  1. Dashboard: Bar Readiness Score, competency profile of only my package subjects, level and XP, learning streak, recommendations.
  2. Subjects: open a subject to see its competency, status, and essays answered.
  3. Syllabi: open the PDF Eis uploaded (View and Download).
  4. Achievements and Badges: badges earned automatically (e.g. First Step to the Bar, ALAC Beginner) and progress toward others.
  5. Open notifications (bell icon) to show Alexa's announcement.
  6. Assessments, Mock Bar: because Alexa set the demo unlock to a 1-day streak, it is unlocked. Show the day picker and how the 20 questions are split by subject. (Default: Level 4 or a 30-day streak.)
Yko: My dashboard tracks my competency per subject and my overall Bar readiness, weighted by the official Bar subject weights. BarBuddy also keeps me engaged with experience points, levels, a daily learning streak and badges. The Mock Bar Examination, twenty questions for one examination day, unlocks once I reach a level or a streak. Laia will now check my answers as a Review Administrator.

PART 4 Laia: Review Administrator (Quality Control) Objectives 2, 3 and 4

4.1 Verifying the AI result

  1. Log in with Laia's Review Administrator account and open Verification. Show the Awaiting Review, Flagged and Lawyer Verified tabs and the AI score column.
  2. Open Yko's well-written essay: question, answer, model answer, AI comparison, AI score and ALAC rating.
  3. Click Mark as Lawyer Verified and confirm.
  4. Open the weaker essay, type an annotation (e.g. "Cite the specific provision of the Revised Corporation Code."), click Flag with Annotation and confirm.
Laia: The AI scores every essay, but a Review Administrator checks every result. If the AI's score and comparison are correct, I mark it as Lawyer Verified. If not, I flag it and add an annotation, which the reviewee sees right away.

4.2 Show the outcome to the reviewee (Yko's browser)

  1. Refresh Yko's results page: one essay shows Lawyer verified, the other shows Laia's annotation.

4.3 Monitoring, badges and logs

  1. Reviewees: readiness, competency, strongest and weakest subjects, streak, last active. Sort, search, and export CSV.
  2. Badges: the badge catalog with conditions. Create a badge or award one to Yko (confirm).
  3. System Logs: Review Administrators can also review the activity log.
Laia: Review Administrators can monitor every reviewee's readiness and weak subjects to guide their review, manage the badges and their award conditions, and check the system logs.
